Descent 3 / DragonBox Pyra - Texture-ID Fix 4
===============================================
Source: Texture-Fix 3, with one targeted correction in
renderer/HardwareOpenGL.cpp.

Root-cause hypothesis, strongly supported by the PowerVR log:
  * GLES2 shader/FBO handles in the run log are 70001 (> 65535).
  * Descent 3 previously cached glGenTextures() GLuint handles in
    uint16_t bitmap/lightmap remap arrays, silently truncating names.
  * glTexSubImage2D was repeatedly failing with GL_INVALID_OPERATION.
  * Truncated names can cause a new/undefined texture to be rebound.

Changes:
  * Change bitmap/lightmap remap entries from uint16_t to GLuint.
  * Allocate remap arrays using sizeof(GLuint).
  * Replace the 65535 sentinel with a full-width invalid GLuint.
  * Keep generated/bound texture IDs as GLuint throughout this path.
  * Log the first twelve native PowerVR glGenTextures() handles at boot.
  * Preserve native EGL/GLES2, SDL3, offscreen presentation, and packed
    16-bit RGBA upload logic from the previous release.

On device:
  ./build-pyra.sh 2>&1 | tee build-pyra.log
  PYRA_GL_TRACE=1 PYRA_GL_CALL_TRACE=1 ./run-pyra.sh 2>&1 | tee run-pyra.log

If the IDs shown by 'PYRA texture id' are > 65535, the truncation is
confirmed as a real defect. The game/menu display must still be verified
on the actual hardware. This archive is not hardware-tested.
